Privacy Policy

Whatifaholic Uploader · Effective 17 November 2026

Who uses this app

Whatifaholic Uploader is a private tool operated by the owner of the Whatifaholic YouTube channel, for that channel only. It is not available to the public and does not collect information from viewers or other people.

What data the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission, granted through Google's sign-in screen, the app uses these Google permissions:

  • YouTube upload (youtube.upload): to upload the channel's own videos.
  • YouTube read-only (youtube.readonly): to confirm which channel it is connected to and read the channel's own video details.
  • YouTube Analytics read-only (yt-analytics.readonly): to read the channel's own performance statistics, such as views and retention.

The app cannot delete videos, comment, or manage anything outside these permissions.

How the data is used

The data is used only to publish the channel's videos and to measure how they perform, so future videos can be improved. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

How the data is stored

The authorization credentials are kept in the channel owner's private settings and are never published. Channel statistics may be stored privately to compare video performance over time.

Google API Services

Whatifaholic Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy. The app uses the YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Once revoked, the app can no longer upload videos or read statistics.
